Executive takeaway

Eleven live research-navigation cases on a Grok Bot machine. Auto-scores that celebrated blocked listing pages were reversed under human QC.

Key finding

Jev is already a strong hands layer when the destination is structured and known. It is not yet a reliable general literature-discovery or download agent. Analytical judgment stays with the researcher — or Quant’s brain suite — after the page lands.

Operating model

Layer 1 · Hands (Jev)

  • Open known DOI, FRED series, EDGAR CIK, clean articles
  • Verify URL / on-page evidence — never trust DONE alone
  • On blocked, hand off to computer-use or human

Layer 2 · Brain (Quant suite)

  • Citation integrity, leakage, nowcast information sets
  • Hard-fail fabricated DOIs
  • File: research_evals/analytical_tool_eval_suite_v1.md

Recommendations

Guidance over enforcement — defaults that help, escape hatches that don’t fight you.

  1. Default Jev for known-URL / portal goals; fall back on blocked, auth, or native GUI.
  2. Prefer DOI and direct series links over open-ended Scholar loops until discovery→landing is stable.
  3. Always verify final URL/text before citing or scoring.
  4. Keep Quant’s analytical suite as the post-navigation quality bar.
